How To Read This Report

  • The throughput tab is a scheduler microbenchmark. Use it to compare scheduler overhead growth, not to claim end-to-end app behavior.
  • The fairness tab is the customer-facing view. Focus on first completion, full completion, and whether non-whales finish ahead of whales.
  • scheduler mode includes: the round-robin scheduler plus a synthetic worker harness that drains scheduled jobs.
  • scheduler mode excludes: queue claiming, notifier app flow, HTTP ingest, and real delivery behavior.

Evidence Boundary

Keep scheduler microbenchmark numbers separate from fairness conclusions. The throughput tab shows component-level scheduler cost. The fairness tab is the right place to judge customer progress behavior.
